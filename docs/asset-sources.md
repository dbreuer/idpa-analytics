## Fonts

Barlow Condensed and DM Sans are subsets of the Google Fonts upstream distributions.
Font binaries and the corresponding Open Font License notices are self-hosted in
`public/fonts/` and `public/licenses/` so page rendering does not depend on a live
font service.

- Barlow Condensed: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/barlowcondensed
- DM Sans: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans
