# Lövésznapló site-wide telemetry system

- **Scope and mode:** Apply the user-selected Steel Challenge telemetry design system to the homepage, all discipline/season dashboards, descriptive reports, methodology pages, unavailable states, navigation, and footer; preserve each surface's existing task and data behavior.
- **Audience and task:** Hungarian sport shooters navigate MDLSZ disciplines, compare season information where validated, and trace results back to official sources.
- **Proof and constraints:** Preserve each discipline's existing ranking or descriptive method, available-year routing, filters, section anchors, official links, data-quality reporting, and factual copy. Do not infer records, measurements, or endorsements not present in the data.
- **Chosen direction:** The user asked to carry the Steel Challenge design across the site. The supplied telemetry asset's charcoal/graphite ground, technical report surfaces, Barlow Condensed titles, orange signal, and restrained green status treatment now form the shared system.
- **THESIS:** Every season page should read as a source-traceable results console while making the discipline's actual analytics method explicit.
- **WORLD:** `#111418` canvas, graphite panels, cool steel rules, off-white text, orange active/ranking states, green only for verified availability, DM Sans for body/interface prose, Barlow Condensed for headings, and monospaced numerals for telemetry.
- **VISITOR PATH:** Discover a discipline, select a season, use its available filters and section navigation, inspect rankings or source-based participation, then follow records to official documents and data-quality details.
- **SIGNATURE INTERACTION:** Shared fixed filters and section navigation stay consistent across analytics dashboards; discipline-specific ranking, chart, and archive behavior remains intact.
- **RESPONSIVE:** Keep the fixed filter/navigation shell usable on phones, collapse data-led grids fluidly, and confine wide official tables to horizontal overflow rather than page overflow.
- **HONEST RISK:** The Steel Challenge reference includes unsupported stage telemetry, national records, time benchmarks, and calculators. The global system adopts its visual language but does not introduce those unsupported claims into other disciplines.
